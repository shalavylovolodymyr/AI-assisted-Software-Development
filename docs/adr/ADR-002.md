# ADR-002 — Explicit demo identity

## Status
Proposed for human review; demo selection explicitly requested by the user.
## Context
The capstone needs actor attribution without pretending to implement enterprise security.
## Alternatives
Anonymous writes, full authentication, or fictional user switching.
## Decision
Show a persistent Demo indicator and selector. Validate X-Demo-User against the directory.
Managers/admins may update actions; members update only their own. Disable writes when demo mode is off.
## Consequences
Good audit demonstrations; unsuitable for exposure to untrusted users. No passwords or tokens.
