# ADR-003 — Immutable assessments and serialized aggregates

## Status
Proposed for human review.
## Context
Resolution must use a risk assessment later than the final required action completion.
## Alternatives
Overwrite risk columns; event sourcing; append-only assessments with transactional aggregates.
## Decision
Append risk records; compute likelihood × impact in the domain. Lock the parent
issue and check versions for every mutation. Preserve cancelled and completed actions.
Require a DONE action for resolution; all-cancelled plans cannot establish mitigation evidence.
## Consequences
Traceable risk trends, clear stale-write errors, PostgreSQL serialization per issue.
